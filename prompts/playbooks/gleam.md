# Playbook: giveaway widgets (Gleam, Rafflecopter, KingSumo, Woobox, ViralSweep)
Entries are a list of actions; each completed action counts. Do the free ones allowed by policy: email entry, visit page, follow on X (policy line), newsletter (record consent). Skip: refer-a-friend, share links, friend tags, uploads of own content, app installs, purchases.
## verify
- Read eligibility in the widget's terms ("Official Rules"/"Terms"): residency or age limits → per the eligibility policy line. Prize physical and shipping local only → residency_required.
- Notes: which entry methods to do and their order. Login with email or the X/Facebook account logged in in the entry browser (sign-up policy line). Lane browser.
## submit
- Gleam: first log in/enter with the profile email (name + email form), then open each allowed entry, complete it and click "Continue"/"Verify"; done when it shows a check mark. Visit entries: open, wait the countdown, close the tab.
- Rafflecopter: "Log in with email", then each option → "Enter".
- KingSumo/Woobox/ViralSweep: the email form is the entry; extra actions only if allowed.
- Captcha: solve nothing automatically; ep owner question.
- Evidence: the entry count or "You're in"/"Entries" text after at least the email entry.
