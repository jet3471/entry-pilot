# Writing guideline for proposals, plans, reports and slides
Applies to every 문서, 기획서, 제안서, PPT. The reader is a judge who knows it is a proposal, so every sentence is written as a confident plan by the one who carries it out.
- State plans and goals as plain statements ("~합니다", "~할 예정입니다", "~를 구축합니다"). No "~로 기대됩니다", "~가능성이 있습니다", "검토할 수 있습니다".
- No sentences about the proposal itself ("this is a draft", "not final", "may change") and no hedging filler ("다만", "일부", "어느 정도").
- Do not answer objections nobody raised, and do not open with a negation before the positive claim. One positive sentence is enough.
- Say a thing once; no "즉", "다시 말해", "요컨대" repeats. End with the strongest claim or the expected result, not an apology, thanks or a promise to improve.
- Facts only from the notice, the user facts file and the profile; never invent results, numbers, partners or schedules. A missing fact: leave the sentence out (never write placeholders such as [확인 필요]); when the work cannot be written without it, `cp owner {{ID}} --question "<the fact needed>"`.
- The submitted file holds the work only: no notes on how it was written, no self-assessment (notes go to SUBMISSION.md).
- Reread once before finishing and delete any sentence that breaks these rules.
