# Playbook: Instagram
The contest engine has no phone and never acts on Instagram: no follow, like, comment, share or post, and no script on instagram.com.
## verify
- The work must be posted on Instagram, or a follow/like/comment is a condition → manual --need sns --reason "<what, which account, post URL>".
- Friend tagging, story repost or sharing required → manual --need sns.
## submit
- Do not open Instagram; manual --need sns --reason "<action, account, post URL>".
