# Playbook: Japanese campaigns (X follow & repost, 応募フォーム, LINE)
- X campaigns: follow the organizer account(s) and repost the campaign post (フォロー＆リポスト); some also ask a reply, a quote with a hashtag, or a like. Use the X playbook selectors and the X policy line. Winners are told by DM, so keep the follow.
- LINE friend-add (友だち追加) campaigns: per the LINE policy line.
- Japanese entry forms (応募フォーム): name, フリガナ (katakana from the profile block), メールアドレス, 電話番号; 郵便番号 / 都道府県 / 住所 required → residency per the policy line.
- Closed: 終了しました / 受付を終了 / 応募受付は終了.
## verify
- Notes: account(s) to follow, campaign post URL, reply/quote text in Japanese (comment rule), form URL if any. Lane browser.
- Instant-win (その場で当たる) campaigns via a third-party app login: login with the shared X account is fine; other accounts per the sign-up policy line.
## submit
- Do the X steps, then the form. Instant-win result pages ("残念"/"当選"): quote the result text as evidence.
